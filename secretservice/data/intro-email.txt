3. Run:  python3 ss-decrypt.py --genkey mykey.key
4. It prints your public key. Reply to this email with that key.

The attached README walks through it step by step, including how to open
a sealed email when one arrives (save the email via "Show original" in
Gmail on a desktop browser, then run the tool on the saved file).

Keep the mykey.key file private. Everything else is safe to forward.
